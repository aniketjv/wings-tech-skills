Common questions

Before you reach out

Is Wings Tech Skills backed by a registered NGO?

Yes. Wings Tech Skills is an initiative by Wings for Dreams, an NGO officially registered with the Government of India (Reg. No. MAH542/2019, PAN: AAATW5579L) holding 80G and 12A tax-exemption certification.

What are the course fees and durations?

Computer Hardware & Chip-Level Repair is 30 days (₹15,000), Enterprise Networking & Infrastructure is 45 days (₹20,000), and the Complete Hardware + Networking Combo is 90 days at a special offer of ₹25,000 – ₹30,000 (instead of ₹35,000). Need-based fee concessions of up to 50% are also available.

Who can get fee assistance?

Fee assistance is need-based. Concessions of up to 50% are considered after an individual discussion with a counselor. The level of support is not automatically guaranteed.

I'm a complete beginner. Can I join?

Yes. We welcome beginners and career seekers from all backgrounds, and we help you learn at a steady pace without judgment.

Where are you and when are you open?

G30 Ground, Ashoka Mall, Bund Garden Rd, Sangamvadi, Pune 411001. We are open Monday to Saturday, 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM, and closed on Sundays.
