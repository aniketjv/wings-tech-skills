Common questions

Before you reach out

Who can get fee assistance?

Fee assistance is need-based. Concessions of up to 100% are considered after an individual discussion with a counselor. The level of support is not automatically guaranteed.

I'm a complete beginner. Can I join?

Yes. We welcome beginners and career seekers from all backgrounds, and we help you learn at a steady pace without judgment.

Do I need to share financial documents in the form?

No. The form only asks for your name and mobile number. Any fee-support discussion happens privately with a counselor.

Can I see the lab before enrolling?

Yes. Book a free practical demo and visit our Pune lab to see the hardware and networking workbenches in person. There is no pressure to enrol.

Where are you and when are you open?

G30 Ground, Ashoka Mall, Bund Garden Rd, Sangamvadi, Pune 411001. We are open Monday to Saturday, 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM, and closed on Sundays.
